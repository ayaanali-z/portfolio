I've gone through maybe six personal websites. A lot of great domain names have gone to waste. Each iteration of my  portfolio has self destructed before any good use could come out of it. It's mainly because I don't actually know why I have a personal portfolio at all. 

I have no published work, beyond some articles in my college's newspaper. I have no academic or professional authority. 

My motivation is further marked with  vissicitudes. The proliferation of AI and the gradual dumbing down of American society into preferring the short, quick, and fast over the slow, deliberate, and careful has increasingly depressed me. 

Yet, this blog exists, and this is the first post. 

I'm not maintaining a schedule or actively seeking to say something unique, critical, or funny. The only good habit that I have consistently (or maybe not so consistently) maintained in my life is reading. And I read in a syllabus for a class I skipped a lot that writing is a reflection of our thinking. A self-diagnosis has provided me with the knowledge that I have trouble sleeping (to quote Ed Norton). That's because I overthink at night. Thus, when I overthink, I'm going to write, and I'm going to write it here. 

I'm against substack because I want complete digital control over my posts, and I don't want it to be algorithmitized by some big tech billioniare. This is going to be a trend in the content. 

I'll write about anything but pattern recognition will predict that much of these posts will be about pop culture (movie, tv, comics), history and my academic life, or in general things that piss me off. 

Hell, I'm writing with the dumbfounded arrogance that people will actually find this corner of the internet and actually read what I post.

Until I become the next [Privacy Dad](https://theprivacydad.com/), this is basically a digital diary. 

Cheers, 

Ayaan
